Which tire brands let us sell without buying stock

We sell online first, with no shop and no warehouse. This sheet ranks the tire brands by how easy they are to start with and what each set earns us. It also says what we still need to ask on the phone.

Researched 11 Oct 2026 · Search demand: Google US monthly average · Margins are planning numbers until a rep sends a price sheet

Lead brands Venom Power + Fury

They likely need no stocking order and publish no price floor, and they cover the 22–30" wheels we're built around.

Add-on Falken

Its rebate program has a category for online sellers, and Keystone sells it with no minimum. Its tires mostly stop at 22".

Later Nitto · Toyo

Nitto: strict price floor, thin margin. Toyo: no website sales without its written OK.

The "24 sets" number

What Nitto actually asks
What we heard
24 sets to be a dealer

That would be 96 tires bought up front and stored, before we've sold one.

What it really is
24 tires, rebates only

It's the opening order for Nitto's rebate program, and online-first sellers can't join that program anyway. Selling Nitto only needs a distributor account. No minimum.

The same holds across brands. The minimums people quote are for rebate programs. Buying one set at a time through a distributor is how every brand below can be sold. Skipping rebates costs us $6–$40 a set.

What people actually search for

Google US, monthly

The Nitto Ridge Grappler gets more searches than Venom, Fury, Toyo, Mickey Thompson and BFGoodrich combined. It's the tire people ask for by name. Venom and Fury win on our wheel sizes, because above 24" the premium brands mostly don't make tires.

What each set makes us

Planning numbers

Budget brands (Venom, Fury) are planned at 20–25% gross. Premium brands (Nitto, Toyo) are planned at 15–20%, because their price floors cap us and online sellers get no rebates. Change the inputs to see what's left.

Freight to a home runs $150–$300 for a set of big tires. Card fee is 2.9% + 30¢ on the set.

TirePer tireSetSet retailGrossFreightCard feeLeft to us

The takeaway: a set of tires on its own barely pays once freight comes out. Inside a wheel package the freight is already paid, and the tires add about $175–$1,250 to the sale. Sell tires as part of the package, not as a separate tire store.

Next steps

In this order
  1. Call Transamerica about Venom Power.Memphis, about 3 hours away. 832-891-2821, M–F 9–6 Central.
  2. Open a Keystone drop-ship account.One account covers Fury and Falken with no minimum order.
  3. Send Fury's dealer inquiry.furytires.com/becomeadistributor. It's a backup if Keystone pricing is thin.
  4. Apply to Turn 14.Accepts web-only dealers and ships wheels with tires mounted and balanced. Ask the buy-in amount first.
  5. Add Nitto and Toyo later.Nitto through a distributor at its price floor. Toyo only after it approves our website.

Ask every rep

None of this is published
  1. Can we buy with no opening order?
  2. Storefront required, or just a resale certificate and business license?
  3. Do you ship single sets to a customer's home? Fee per order, freight per set? Sets of six for duallys?
  4. Do you mount and balance onto wheels?
  5. Dealer cost against suggested retail on our top four sizes?
  6. Written price-floor policy? Can we discount inside a wheel package?
  7. How do warranty claims work for an online seller with no shop?
  8. Does a fit inside your published rim-width range keep the warranty?
  9. Lead time and stock on 24", 26" and 28" sizes?
  10. Load data per part number as a spec sheet or feed?
  11. Can we use your photos and logo, in writing?
  12. Which distributors carry your full big-wheel range?

Still unknown

Answered by the calls above
  • Real dealer cost. Every margin here is a planning range until a price sheet arrives.
  • Whether Venom or Fury want an opening order. Neither publishes one.
  • Turn 14's buy-in amount, and whether it takes a brand-new business.
  • Venom's warranty at 24" and 26". Its mileage warranty only covers 22" and smaller, and claims come back to us as the seller.
