01

Forged Chrome

Dark gunmetal base, beveled perimeter, bright chrome edge top-left, darker underside, metallic grain, inner shadow. Hover: a sharp chrome reflection sweeps diagonally. Click: it depresses.

Bevel is four inset shadows, not a border — light on the top-left, black on the underside, plus an inner shadow for depth. Grain is an SVG turbulence layer at 5%.

03

Floating Chrome Plate

Polished billet floating above black. Thin chrome perimeter, no obvious gradient on the face, large soft shadow. Hover: the plate rises and the edge brightens.

The perimeter is a 1px gradient ring via mask-composite:exclude, so the chrome runs bright at the top and falls to graphite at the bottom like a real machined edge.

05

Chrome Liquid

Liquid chrome poured into a shape — irregular reflections rather than one uniform gradient. Hover: the chrome flows toward your cursor.

Four offset radial gradients break up the mirror; a fifth tracks the pointer through --mx/--my. The only concept here that needs JavaScript.

07

Floating Center Cap

A premium cap sitting above the page. Polished outer ring, black center, embossed mark, deep bevel. Hover: the cap turns and the ring catches a highlight.

Ring is a conic-gradient — that is what gives a turned-metal look rather than a plastic one. The round one rotates and counter-rotates its mark so the label stays upright; the elongated one only lifts, because rotating a word reads as broken rather than premium.

Polished Lip

A black face inside a deep polished wheel lip — bright silver at one point, darkening around. Hover: the highlight travels all the way around the lip.

A 4px conic-gradient ring with an animated angle (@property), so the bright spot genuinely orbits the button instead of sliding across it. Closest thing here to holding an actual barrel.

★

The light moves, not the button

A wheel under a showroom light doesn't move — the light travels across it. At rest: gunmetal with barely visible machining. Hover: one narrow, high-intensity chrome reflection crosses it in ~520ms, then it settles back. Click: 2px depression, then the sweep.

This is a finish, not a colour — the same sweep works on the pale face on the right, which is why it could run across the whole system instead of one button. The machining is a 3px repeating gradient at under 3% opacity; you read it rather than see it.
