Why forged

A forged wheel costs more and takes weeks, and here is what you get for it

A cast wheel is molten aluminum poured into a mold. A forged wheel starts as a solid block of aluminum pressed into shape under thousands of tons of force, then machined down to the design. Pressing packs the grain tight, so the wheel carries more strength for its weight. That counts on a three-quarter or one-ton truck running wide wheels at a deep offset, and plenty of these trucks still pull a trailer on the weekend.

Sizing

What the width and offset do to the way your truck sits

Diameter sets how much tire sidewall you keep. Width and offset set how far the wheel sits out past the fender, which is the part people mean when they say poke.

A wider wheel almost always runs a more negative offset, and that pushes the tire out. Past a point it means a lift, some trimming or a different tire, and that is the conversation we have with you before anything gets cut.

Questions

What JTX buyers ask us before they order

How long does a JTX set take?

JTX build every set after the order is placed, so it takes weeks rather than days. The exact lead time depends on the style, size and finish, and it is written on your quote before you pay anything.

How do I know the wheels will fit my truck?

We check the bolt pattern, center bore, offset and brake clearance for your exact year, make and model, then confirm the spec with JTX before they machine anything. If a size you want needs a lift, trimming or a different tire, we tell you on the quote.

Can I tow on forged wheels?

Yes, as long as the load rating covers what you pull. Tell us your trailer weight when you ask for a quote. If a size or style comes up short for that weight, we say so and point you to one that covers it.

Why does a forged set cost this much?

Each wheel is pressed from a solid aluminum block and machined to your order, one set at a time. You are paying for the strength, the size you picked and a finish nobody else's truck came off the lot with. Prices on this page start from JTX's base sizes, and your quote prices the exact build.

Can I pay over time?

Financing is available on approved credit. Ask for it on your quote and we send the options for your build.

Can you add tires?

Yes. Tell us the tire size or the look you want and we match a tire to the wheel width. We sell tires from every major brand and quote the package together.

What if I want a style that isn't on this page?

Every one of the 154 JTX styles is in the builder above under "Show all". If you have a photo of a build you like, send it with your quote request.
