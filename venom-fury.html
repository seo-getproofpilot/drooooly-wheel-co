Venom Power and Fury: what it takes to sell them

These are our two lead tire brands. Neither publishes a stocking requirement or a price floor, so we can sell set by set with no inventory. This sheet covers how to get each account, what we'd earn, which tire fits which wheel, and the gaps to close on the phone.

How a tire order works

Same setup as Price Designs

Decision: sell tires from launch, on the same quote as the wheels, so customers buy the whole setup in one place. Tires and wheels ship separately and arrive unmounted; the customer's local shop mounts them.

It isn't a percentage of the sale. We buy at dealer cost, sell at our own price, and keep the difference. Venom or Fury never pays us. We're the store; they're the supplier that ships for us.

  1. The customer approves the quote and pays us for wheels and tires.Collect the tire money before we order.
  2. We order from Venom or Fury at our dealer price, shipped to the customer's address.Freight goes on the quote as its own line. It's never "free shipping."
  3. They ship straight to the customer.We pass the tracking number along.
  4. We pay the brand.By card or bank transfer at first; 30-day terms usually come after a few months of history.

Because we're the store, these are ours

  • Sales tax. We collect it, same as on the wheels.
  • Our price. No published price floor, so we set it. Check Custom Offsets so we're not the most expensive.
  • Problems. Wrong tire, shipping damage, warranty claims all come to us, and we deal with the brand.
  • Cash timing. We may pay the brand before the customer's money clears. Taking payment first avoids it.
Not confirmed yet

That Venom or Fury will ship a single set to a customer's home. Online retailers selling their tires suggests yes, but neither brand says so anywhere. Ask it first on both calls: "Will you ship a set to my customer's home, and what does that cost?" If not, Fury still works through Meyer, which ships to customers.

Tell the customer on the product page and in the order confirmation: the tires and wheels arrive separately and unmounted, and need a local shop to mount and balance them. If a dually customer says they tow, the fitment hard stop applies.

The low-profile street look

A smaller, separate crowd runs street tires: 255/30R22 (390 searches a month), 255/30R24 (320), 255/35R22 (140) and 255/30R26 (110). Venom's Ragnarok GTS covers 24", 26" and 28" (255/30R24, 255/30R26, 255/30R28) through the same Transamerica account. If street duallys take off, add Delinte DS8 (24–30", US warehouses) or Turbo Wholesale, where one account gets Lexani, Lionhart and RBP ("Lexani tires" 12,100/mo, "Lionhart tires" 6,600/mo).

These are passenger tires rated about 1,600–2,000 lb each, with no dual rating. They're for the look, not for a working truck.

Two things to ask both brands

Rim width: these tall tires are approved for rims from 8.5", and dually wheels are 8.25". Sellers do this all the time, but Fury's warranty excludes fitments outside its guidelines, so ask whether an 8.25" dually wheel is covered. Six-tire sets: will they ship a set of six to a home?

Recommendation: no new brand for duallys at launch. Venom and Fury cover the off-road dually look at every size, and Venom's Ragnarok covers the street look on 24–28". Add Delinte or Turbo only if street-style dually orders start coming in. If a dually customer says they tow, the fitment hard stop still applies.

Warranty: what lands on us

Both brands send claims to the seller

Venom Power

  • Mileage warranty: 50,000 miles, but only on 22" and smaller, up to 13.5" wide. It's also void when the size doesn't match the owner's manual, which on a lifted truck likely means none.
  • Defects: 4 years, not prorated.
  • Road hazard: free for 12 months, but not off-road, over 1-ton or commercial.
  • Claims: online buyers return them to the seller, which is us.

Fury

  • Term: none published. 4 years is reported. No mileage figures anywhere.
  • Payout: full value in the first 1/32" of wear, then prorated.
  • Excluded: off-road, staggered, stretched and "oversized" fitments. Widest approved rim is 15.5".
  • Claims: only through the selling dealer, which is us. The customer pays mounting and freight.

For us: sell only inside each brand's published rim range, road-force balance every set, and register every tire for the customer. Federal rules require independent dealers to give each buyer a way to register. Both brands have online forms: transamericatire.com/tire_registration and furytires.com/registration.

Call scripts

Ten questions each

Venom · 832-891-2821

  1. Do you sell direct to an online-only dealer? Is there an opening order or a yearly minimum?
  2. Will you ship sets to the customer's home under our name? Fee per order?
  3. Which warehouse ships to Tennessee, and how fast? Is freight free above a quantity?
  4. Dealer price list and volume tiers. Is federal excise tax included?
  5. Is there a price floor (MAP) policy? Can we discount inside a wheel package?
  6. Can we sell on Amazon, eBay and Walmart too?
  7. Is an 8.25" dually wheel covered with your 12.50 and 13.50 tires? Do you ship sets of six to a home?
  8. Full spec sheets (load, psi, weight) for 20–30" sizes, ideally as a data feed?
  9. Does the "size not per owner's manual" rule void the warranty on lifted trucks?
  10. Can we use your photos and logo? Who is our rep, and is Tennessee open?

Fury · (855) 575-3879

  1. Do you sell direct to an online-only Tennessee dealer, or only through Keystone, Meyer or OVJ?
  2. Dealer cost on 24–30" sizes, volume tiers, and any opening order?
  3. Is there a price floor (MAP)? Can we show prices on our site?
  4. Do you ship sets of 4 and 6 to homes? Cost, warehouse and days? How do 30" tires ship?
  5. Is an 8.25" dually wheel covered with your 12.50 and 13.50 tires? Sets of six to a home? Any dual load ratings?
  6. Can you correct the load figures that disagree on your spec pages, in writing?
  7. What counts as "stretched" or "oversized"? Is any fit inside your rim range always covered?
  8. Mileage warranty per model, and is 4 years current?
  9. How do claims work for a seller with no shop: photos, returns, freight?
  10. Can we use your photos? Is there a co-op or marketing program?
