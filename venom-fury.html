Venom Power and Fury: what it takes to sell them

These are our two lead tire brands. Neither publishes a stocking requirement or a price floor, so we can sell set by set with no inventory. This sheet covers how to get each account, what we'd earn, which tire fits which wheel, and the gaps to close on the phone.

The dually gap

Ask both brands
Neither brand covers HD duallys on 22–26x8.25 wheels

A dually's inner and outer rear tires need a dual load rating. Neither brand publishes one for any flotation size (33x, 35x, 37x) or for anything on a 24"+ rim. Venom's only tires that fit 22–26x8.25 are low-profile street tires rated about 1,500–1,700 lb, far too light for a 3500.

What does exist: dual-rated LT sizes on 20" and some 22" wheels. These are Venom R/T+ LT275/65R20 (fits 20x8.25), and Fury R/T and Sierra Hunter ATM in LT285/55R20, LT305/55R20, LT285/50R22 and LT285/55R22.

Until a rep sends dual ratings in writing, DROOOLY's dually packages on 22"+ need a third tire source. That's the one place we'll have to look beyond these two brands.

Warranty: what lands on us

Both brands send claims to the seller

Venom Power

  • Mileage warranty: 50,000 miles, but only on 22" and smaller, up to 13.5" wide. It's also void when the size doesn't match the owner's manual, which on a lifted truck likely means none.
  • Defects: 4 years, not prorated.
  • Road hazard: free for 12 months, but not off-road, over 1-ton or commercial.
  • Claims: online buyers return them to the seller, which is us.

Fury

  • Term: none published. 4 years is reported. No mileage figures anywhere.
  • Payout: full value in the first 1/32" of wear, then prorated.
  • Excluded: off-road, staggered, stretched and "oversized" fitments. Widest approved rim is 15.5".
  • Claims: only through the selling dealer, which is us. The customer pays mounting and freight.

For us: sell only inside each brand's published rim range, road-force balance every set, and register every tire for the customer. Federal rules require independent dealers to give each buyer a way to register. Both brands have online forms: transamericatire.com/tire_registration and furytires.com/registration.

Call scripts

Ten questions each

Venom · 832-891-2821

  1. Do you sell direct to an online-only dealer? Is there an opening order or a yearly minimum?
  2. Will you ship sets to the customer's home under our name? Fee per order?
  3. Which warehouse ships to Tennessee, and how fast? Is freight free above a quantity?
  4. Dealer price list and volume tiers. Is federal excise tax included?
  5. Is there a price floor (MAP) policy? Can we discount inside a wheel package?
  6. Can we sell on Amazon, eBay and Walmart too?
  7. Any dual-rated LT sizes for 22–26x8.25 dually wheels, now or coming?
  8. Full spec sheets (load, psi, weight) for 20–30" sizes, ideally as a data feed?
  9. Does the "size not per owner's manual" rule void the warranty on lifted trucks?
  10. Can we use your photos and logo? Who is our rep, and is Tennessee open?

Fury · (855) 575-3879

  1. Do you sell direct to an online-only Tennessee dealer, or only through Keystone, Meyer or OVJ?
  2. Dealer cost on 24–30" sizes, volume tiers, and any opening order?
  3. Is there a price floor (MAP)? Can we show prices on our site?
  4. Do you ship sets of 4 and 6 to homes? Cost, warehouse and days? How do 30" tires ship?
  5. Dual load ratings for 35x12.50R20/22, 37x13.50R22 and LT405/30R26?
  6. Can you correct the load figures that disagree on your spec pages, in writing?
  7. What counts as "stretched" or "oversized"? Is any fit inside your rim range always covered?
  8. Mileage warranty per model, and is 4 years current?
  9. How do claims work for a seller with no shop: photos, returns, freight?
  10. Can we use your photos? Is there a co-op or marketing program?
