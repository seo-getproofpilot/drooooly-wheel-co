Sell both
from launch

Both brands can be sold set by set with no inventory and no published buy-in. We set our own price because neither publishes a price floor. Tires go on the same quote as the wheels, so customers buy the whole setup in one place, and each set adds roughly $200–$1,500 in margin to a sale we're already making.

Which tire for each of our wheels

Inside each brand's approved rim width

Every wheel size we sell, 20–30", with each brand's go-to tire that is approved for that rim width, so the warranty holds. The number shows how many other sizes from that brand also fit.

Our wheelVenom Power go-to tireFury go-to tire

16"-wide wheels: neither brand approves any tire for a 22, 24, 26 or 28x16 wheel. The widest approvals are 15.5" (Venom Swampthing 35x15.50R24, Fury LT405/30R26). Only a 30x16 has a match, Venom's Swampthing 42 and 44x16.50R30. Anything else on a 16" wheel is outside the brand's warranty.

Load: a stock heavy-duty tire carries about 3,640 lb. Many 24–26" picks carry less, which is where our towing hard stop applies.

Dually builds

What people run on 8.25" dually wheels

Big-wheel dually builds run tall off-road tires, mostly 35x12.50 and 37x13.50 in 12-ply, not low-profile street tires. These tires are approved for rims from 8.5", and dually wheels are 8.25". Sellers run them that way all the time, but ask both brands whether it keeps the warranty.

How a tire order works

Same setup as Price Designs

It isn't a percentage of the sale. We buy at dealer cost, sell at our own price, and keep the difference. Venom or Fury never pays us; we're the store and they ship for us. Tires and wheels arrive separately and unmounted, and the customer's local shop mounts them.

  1. The customer approves the quote and pays us for wheels and tires.We collect the tire money before ordering.
  2. We order from Venom or Fury at dealer price, shipped to the customer.Freight is its own line on the quote, never "free shipping."
  3. They ship straight to the customer.We pass along the tracking number.
  4. We pay the brand.By card or bank transfer at first; 30-day terms usually come after a few months.

Ours to handle

  • Sales tax. We collect it, same as on wheels.
  • Our price. Check Custom Offsets so we're not the most expensive.
  • Problems. Wrong tire, shipping damage and warranty claims come to us.
  • Tell the customer on the product page and order confirmation that tires and wheels arrive separately and unmounted.

What would change the plan

  • An opening order we'd have to buy and store.
  • No home delivery. Fury still works through Meyer.
  • Dealer cost well above our assumption, leaving under about 15%.
  • Frequent warranty claims on the budget sizes.
