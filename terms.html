How we work

We’re an independent shop. We don’t take payment on this site, we don’t publish prices, and apart from one brand we aren’t anybody’s authorized dealer yet. All of that is below in plain language.

Who we are

These Terms of Sale are between you and DROOOLY Wheel & Tire LLC, a Tennessee limited liability company (“DROOOLY,” “we,” “us,” “our”), 5521 S York Hwy, Grimsley, TN 38565.

Independent retailer

DROOOLY Wheel & Tire is an independent seller of aftermarket wheels and tires. We are not affiliated with, sponsored by, or endorsed by the manufacturers whose products appear on this site unless we say so on that brand’s page.

Price Designs is an authorized dealer line. Their pages carry an “Authorized dealer” badge. No other brand on this site carries one today, and we will not claim otherwise.

Trademarks and images

All brand names, logos, product names, and product images are the property of their respective owners. They appear here only to identify products we can source for our customers. Their use does not imply any partnership or endorsement.

Removal requests

If you represent a brand and would prefer your name, logo, or products not appear on this site, email us at chris@drooolywt.com, call or text 602-332-5400, or use our contact form. We’ll remove the listing promptly.

No payment online

Adding wheels or tires to your build and sending it does not charge you or place an order. We confirm fitment, availability, and final pricing with you and send a quote. Nothing is ordered and no payment is taken until you approve that quote.

Pricing and availability

We don’t list prices on this site. Pricing comes in your quote. Sizes, finishes, and availability shown here may change and are confirmed in that quote.

Fitment

We verify fitment before we build a set — we don’t guarantee that a wheel will bolt up sight unseen. If you tell us what you tow or haul and the assembly’s load rating falls short, we will say so and talk it through rather than take the order.
