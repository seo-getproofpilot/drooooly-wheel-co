# Venom Power and Fury — what it takes to sell them

Chris: *"Venom Power and Fury are going to make the most sense … let's dig deeper
into what it takes for Venom Power and Fury and throw the others out the window."*

Follows `TIRE-SOURCING.md`, which compared eight brands and picked these two. This
file covers only Venom and Fury. The presentable version for Matthew is the
"Venom & Fury Playbook" sheet (claude.ai/artifact/8YhswQTxiFPiz6cPdY12vn).

Labels: **[O]** official (brand or distributor's own page), **[R]** reported,
**[I]** inferred. Researched 2026-10-11.

Short version:

- **Neither brand publishes an opening order, a stocking requirement or a price
  floor (MAP).** Retail prices vary widely between sellers, which supports that [I].
  Selling set by set with no inventory looks realistic, but only the two calls
  confirm it.
- **How they split:**
  - **Venom leads on 20–24x12.** It's cheaper, more searched ("venom tires"
    6,600/mo, "venom power tires" 4,400) and carries similar load.
  - **Fury leads on 24x14 and every 26–30" wheel**, where its tires carry more load.
- **Venom is bought direct from Transamerica.** No wholesaler carries it. Every
  online seller on Venom's dealer list buys direct [I].
- **Fury has three routes in:** direct, Meyer, or Keystone. **Keystone requires a
  website pre-approved with a working checkout** [O]. `PAYMENTS.md` plans
  quote-first until January, so Keystone waits.
- **The real gap is HD duallys.** Neither brand publishes a dual load rating for
  any flotation size or for anything on a 24"+ rim. On 22/24/26x8.25 dually wheels,
  Venom's only fits are street tires at about 1,500–1,700 lb. Dually packages on
  22"+ need a third source until a rep says otherwise.

**Warranty:**
- **Mileage:** 50,000 miles on the X/T, X/T2, R/T and R/T+, but only on **22" and
  smaller, up to 13.5" wide**.

**Freight:**
- Tires 35" and up carry parcel oversize surcharges of about **$30–$60 a tire**,
  plus a residential fee [I].
- A 44x16.50R30 is at FedEx Ground's size limit and may need truck freight.
- Inside a wheel package the freight is already paid, so the tire margin is close
  to clear profit.
- Federal excise tax on the heaviest tires is $4–$8 each, built into the brand's
  price [I, 26 U.S.C. 4071].

## 5. Before calling, have ready

- EIN and business license
- Tennessee sales-tax number
- Resale certificate
- Website URL
- An honest volume estimate (under $100K / under 1,000 tires)
- Shipping plan (customer or partner installer)
- How we handle warranty claims (by photo)
- A trade reference (Price Designs)

## 6. Call scripts

**Venom, 832-891-2821** (Joe Davis / Steve Oropesa)
1. Direct to an online-only dealer? Opening order or yearly minimum?
2. Ship sets to the customer's home under our name? Fee per order?
3. Which warehouse ships to Tennessee, how fast? Free freight above a quantity?
4. Dealer price list, volume tiers. Is federal excise tax included?
5. Is there a MAP policy? Can we discount inside a wheel package?
6. Amazon, eBay, Walmart allowed?
7. Dual-rated LT sizes for 22–26x8.25 dually wheels, now or coming?
8. Full spec sheets (load, psi, weight) for 20–30", as a feed?
9. Does "size not per owner's manual" void the warranty on lifted trucks?
10. Photo and logo permission in writing. Who's our rep? Is Tennessee open?

**Fury, (855) 575-3879** (Ernie Boehm)
1. Direct to an online-only Tennessee dealer, or only through Keystone, Meyer or OVJ?
2. Dealer cost on 24–30", tiers, opening order?
3. Is there a MAP policy? Can we show prices?
4. Ship sets of 4 and 6 to homes? Cost, warehouse, days? How do 30" tires ship?
5. Dual load ratings for 35x12.50R20/22, 37x13.50R22, LT405/30R26?
6. Written corrections for the mismatched load figures in §2?
7. Definitions of "stretched" and "oversized". Is any fit inside your rim range covered?
8. Mileage warranty per model; is 4 years current?
9. Claims for a seller with no shop: photos, returns, freight?
10. Photo permission; co-op or marketing program?
